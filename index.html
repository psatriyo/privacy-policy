Privacy Policy for RandomCreator

Last Updated: November 6, 2025

Introduction

RandomCreator ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how RandomCreator handles your information when you use our iOS application.

Our Privacy Commitment

RandomCreator does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal information. The app is designed to work entirely offline and all data remains on your device.

Information Storage

Local Data Only

RandomCreator stores the following information locally on your device only using Apple's standard UserDefaults system:

  • Number Randomizer Settings: Your preferred minimum and maximum number range (1-10,000)
  • Name Lists: Up to 10 names you add to the Name Randomizer
  • Dice Settings: Your preferred number of dice (1-10) and sides per die (2-120)
  • App Preferences: Your default randomizer mode selection

No Cloud Storage

  • No data is synced to iCloud or any cloud service
  • No data leaves your device
  • All settings and data remain private to your device

Data We Do NOT Collect

RandomCreator does not collect, access, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers (UDID, advertising ID, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Crash reports
  • Any data about your randomization results or history
  • Contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Network or browsing activity

No Third-Party Access

  • No third-party services: We do not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking services
  • No data sharing: Your data is never shared with, sold to, or accessed by third parties
  • No advertising: The app contains no advertisements or advertising trackers
  • No network connections: The app operates completely offline and makes no network requests

Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • Device-level security: All data is protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, encryption)
  • Local storage only: Data never leaves your device, eliminating transmission security risks
  • No external access: No servers or databases that could be compromised

Your Data Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

Delete Your Data

You can delete all app data by:

  • Opening the Settings tab in the app
  • Using the "Reset Settings" option (if available), or
  • Deleting the app from your device (removes all app data)

Export Your Data

Currently, the app does not support exporting name lists or settings. All data remains on your device.

Data Portability

If you delete and reinstall the app, all previous settings and name lists will be lost as they are not backed up to iCloud.

Children's Privacy

RandomCreator does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for all ages and contains no advertising, in-app purchases, or external links.

Device Permissions

RandomCreator may request the following permissions:

  • Motion & Fitness (CoreMotion): Used only to detect shake gestures for the Dice Randomizer feature. No motion data is stored or transmitted.
  • Haptic Feedback (CoreHaptics): Used to provide tactile feedback when generating random results. No data is collected.

These permissions are used solely for app functionality and no data is collected or transmitted.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's functionality. We will notify users of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Including a notice in the app update notes

Compliance

RandomCreator is designed to comply with:

  • Apple's App Store Review Guidelines
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Since no data is collected or transmitted, most privacy regulations do not apply to this app.

Summary

In Plain English:

  • RandomCreator stores your settings only on your phone
  • Nothing is sent to the internet or any servers
  • We don't know who you are or how you use the app
  • Delete the app = delete all data
  • Your privacy is 100% protected because we never see your information

Your privacy matters. RandomCreator keeps everything local, always.
